# Liquor Store Lease Search: Lexington / Red Bank / Forest Acres, SC

Research date: 2026-09-28. Listings come from search-result snippets, so check each one with the broker before you rely on it.

## Legal filter to apply first (SC retail liquor)
- **300 ft** from any church, school or playground inside city limits (Lexington town, Forest Acres).
- **500 ft** outside city limits (much of Red Bank is unincorporated Lexington County).
- Distance is measured from the store's nearest entrance along the shortest public pedestrian/vehicle route to the nearest entrance of the church or school grounds.
- **No waivers for retail liquor stores.** The 2014 church waiver in 61-6-120(C) applies only to on-premises licenses.
- The license comes from SCDOR, and local zoning must also allow the use.

## Next steps
1. For each site, map churches, schools and playgrounds within 300/500 ft.
2. Confirm zoning and that the landlord allows liquor sales. Some centers have exclusives or restrictions.
3. Check the SC liquor store limit of 3 licenses per person/entity if relevant.
4. Get a lease contingency for SCDOR license approval.
