Retail liquor · Lexington, Red Bank & Forest Acres, SC

Liquor Store Site Shortlist

A first pass at available retail spaces that could hold a package liquor store. Listing details were checked against public listings on September 29, 2026. Rents marked "on request" need a call to the broker.

What every site has to pass

  • Distance: no church, school or playground within 300 ft inside city limits, or 500 ft outside city limits. Measured along the road or sidewalk from the store's entrance. SC does not allow waivers for liquor stores.
  • Zoning: the parcel's zoning must allow retail liquor sales. Forest Acres banned new alcohol sales in the Covenant Crossing area (April 2026). None of the sites below are in that area.
  • Lease: the landlord must allow liquor sales, and no other tenant can hold an exclusive on it.

Distances below have not been measured yet. They are the first thing to confirm on each site.
