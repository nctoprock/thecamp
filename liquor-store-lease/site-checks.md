## Zoning notes by area
- **Town of Lexington** (Sunset Blvd sites): the town's use table lists which districts allow retail; alcohol rules are in Code Chapter 90. The three existing liquor stores on Sunset Blvd show general commercial zoning there allows it. Call Lexington Planning & Zoning to confirm the district for your exact parcel.
- **Red Bank** (unincorporated Lexington County): county zoning ordinance applies (latest version dated 6/23/2026). The **500 ft** rule applies if the parcel is outside town limits. Lexington County Zoning: 803-785-8121.
- **Forest Acres:** the April 14, 2026 amendment bans new alcohol and vape sales in the **Covenant Crossing** overlay (around Covenant Rd and Bethel Church Rd near Trenholm Park). The city is also weighing a Forest Drive corridor overlay (drive-thru ban, size limits). Call Forest Acres Planning & Zoning to confirm your parcel's district.

## Open questions and who to call
1. **Is a daycare a "school"** under the SC distance rule? This matters for the Forest Dr sites because of Kids Garden at 4714 Forest Dr. Ask SCDOR or an alcohol-license attorney.
2. **Exclusives:** ask each broker in writing whether any tenant has an exclusive on liquor, beer or wine, and whether the lease bans package liquor stores.
3. **Get a measurement:** have the broker or a surveyor measure the walking/driving route from the suite door to the nearest church, school or playground entrance.
4. **Lease contingency:** make the lease depend on SCDOR license approval and zoning sign-off.
