About Lori

Lori is the CEO & Founder of the @Home Team, a Managing Broker at Keller Williams Palmetto, and a licensed Real Estate Instructor — with over 30 years across sales, marketing, and training. Her career began in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, where she earned her degree in Liberal Arts from Broward College and cut her teeth in property management and real estate advertising before moving into sales.

Today, deeply rooted in the South Carolina market, Lori has built a professional network spanning clients, investors, builders, and community leaders — the kind of relationships that turn a transaction into a long-term advantage for the people she represents.
